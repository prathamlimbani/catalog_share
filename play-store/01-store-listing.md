## Graphics you must supply

Play will not let you publish without these.

| Asset | Spec | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **App icon** | 512 × 512 PNG, 32-bit, under 1 MB | ✅ `public/icon-512.png` is 512×512 — verify it has no transparency and no rounded corners baked in |
| **Feature graphic** | 1024 × 500 PNG/JPG, no transparency | ❌ **Must be created** — appears at the top of your listing |
| **Phone screenshots** | 2–8 images, 16:9 or 9:16, each 320–3840 px on the short side | ❌ **Must be captured** |
| **7" tablet screenshots** | Up to 8 | Optional — only if you list tablet support |
| **10" tablet screenshots** | Up to 8 | Optional |
| **Promo video** | YouTube URL | Optional |

**Screenshot suggestions** — capture these six screens on a phone:
1. Storefront as a customer sees it (the strongest first impression)
2. Admin dashboard with the store link and copy button
3. Product add/edit screen with images
4. A finished estimate/invoice PDF
5. Cart → WhatsApp order handoff
6. Analytics screen

Tip: add a one-line caption band across the top of each screenshot. Plain unannotated screenshots convert noticeably worse.
